About Bridgit

Bridgit McKinney writes emotionally charged paranormal romance, dark fantasy, and mythological fiction where powerful bloodlines, complicated families, ancient rivalries, and unforgettable love stories collide. Her books blend high-stakes supernatural worlds with sharp humor, deeply human characters, and the kind of relationships that leave scars, start wars, and change entire legacies.

At the heart of everything she writes is a love of storytelling and a fascination with twisting fact, myth, history, and familiar ideas into something unexpected. Bridgit has a unique way of looking at a situation from angles other people often do not consider, then asking the question that changes the entire story.

From werewolf packs and fated mates to gods, monsters, and divine families behaving very badly, her stories are built around bold choices, complicated loyalties, and characters forced to see their world differently. She is especially drawn to the moments where love, fear, power, family, and identity collide in ways that are messy, funny, dangerous, and deeply human.

Based in the Appalachian region, Bridgit brings a grounded, fiercely personal voice to larger-than-life worlds. Whether she is writing about an Alpha fighting for his mate, ancient bloodlines colliding, or Greek gods trying to survive their own children, she tells stories with heart, humor, and a perspective all her own.

Bridgit is the author of multiple paranormal, fantasy, and mythological titles, with more stories always waiting in the wings.
