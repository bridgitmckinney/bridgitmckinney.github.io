About Bridgit

Bridgit McKinney writes paranormal romance from the hills of Appalachian Ohio, where the mountains, the back roads, and the people who live along them find their way into everything she writes.

Her stories are about wolves, family, second chances, and characters stubborn enough to earn their happy endings.

When she is not writing, she is working a full-time job and plotting the next book.
