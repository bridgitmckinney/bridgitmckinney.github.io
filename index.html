Meet Bridgit

Bridgit McKinney writes emotionally charged paranormal romance, dark fantasy, and mythological fiction where powerful bloodlines, complicated families, ancient rivalries, and unforgettable love stories collide. Her books blend high-stakes supernatural worlds with sharp humor, deeply human characters, and the kind of relationships that leave scars, start wars, and change entire legacies.

Based in the Appalachian region, Bridgit brings a grounded, fiercely personal voice to larger-than-life worlds. Whether she is writing about an Alpha fighting for his mate, ancient bloodlines colliding, or Greek gods trying to survive their own children, she tells stories with heart, humor, and a perspective all her own.
