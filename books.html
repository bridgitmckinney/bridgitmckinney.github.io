The Alpha’s Brother

A werewolf romance. Coming soon.

Ryder Kane has a platinum record, a Harley, and a reputation that gets to the room before he does. He is also the younger brother of the Alpha of Black Heart Pack, the largest and strongest wolf pack in the United States, and the only wolf in line for a title he never wanted.

The road was supposed to keep him free. Then fate hands him a mate who breaks him in front of the whole world, and a second chance he does not trust: Kira Vane, the smart-mouthed lead singer opening his stadium tour, who is not afraid of his name, his wolf, or his past.

A country star. A pack a thousand wolves strong. And a woman who intends to keep him.

Rook

A werewolf novel. Available now from Amazon and other booksellers.

Damien Steel was born to inherit Bloodmoon, and everyone expected his first shift to confirm exactly what he was destined to become. Instead, at eighteen, his shift fails, because the wolf waiting inside him is nothing like anyone expected.

Rook is ancient, sovereign, merciless, and tied to the oldest Bloodmoon bloodlines. He carries a power that does not belong in the modern world, and he has absolutely no intention of submitting to Damien simply because they share the same body.

If Damien wants control, he will have to earn it, and Rook intends to make him bleed for every inch. What begins as a vicious battle of wills becomes years of brutal training, war, loyalty, betrayal, loss, and the slow forging of a bond neither man nor wolf believed they would ever want.

Rook becomes Damien’s harshest teacher, his deadliest weapon, and eventually the one being he trusts without question. Then Chloe Carter walks into their life and changes everything.

She is stubborn, sharp-tongued, impossible to intimidate, and determined to uncover secrets Bloodmoon has protected for generations. Rook knows what she is the moment he senses her, while Damien understands just as quickly how dangerous loving her could become.

She is their mate, and their enemies know exactly what that means. When Chloe is pulled into a threat rooted in Bloodmoon’s past, Damien will have to decide whether controlling Rook was ever truly the answer or whether saving the woman they love will require him to finally stop fighting the monster within.

Before Damien Steel became a legend, there was the battle that nearly broke him and the wolf who refused to kneel. This is the story of Rook, the creature who was never meant to be controlled and the man who had to become strong enough to stand beside him.

Where the Wild Sky Breaks

A Montana romance. Available now from Amazon and other booksellers.

Montana, 1889. Six years after leaving the West, Caelon Caldwell returns to Blue Sky Ranch wealthier, harder, and determined to build the life he once believed he had to leave behind. Shiloh Dutton is no longer the eighteen-year-old girl who trusted him with her heart and watched his letters slowly disappear; she is a capable rancher in her own right, fiercely independent and unwilling to surrender herself simply because the first man she ever loved has finally come home.

But just as the future they once dreamed about finally seems possible, a woman from Caelon’s life in Boston arrives in Montana with intentions of her own, bringing his past directly to Shiloh’s doorstep. Even after vows are spoken and a home becomes theirs, one carefully planted doubt threatens everything they have rebuilt, leaving Shiloh with a fear no proof will ever fully settle.

This is a complete love story with an earned, deeply satisfying happily-ever-after for two people who discover that sometimes the wildest thing a heart can do is trust again.

A Wolf Remembers

Pale Strand, book one. Available now in ebook and paperback.

For generations, the wolves lived by truths handed down so faithfully that no one thought to question where those truths began. They knew the gods who watched over them, the rites that bound them, the names that carried their history forward, and the laws that told them what they were. Then Wulfwyn began asking why, and one question became the first loose thread in a history far older and more dangerous than anyone understood.

What she uncovers is not a forgotten legend but a buried identity, one powerful enough to unsettle wolves across the world and force the gods themselves to confront what was lost, what was stolen, and what they allowed to remain hidden. Ancient loyalties fracture as packs are pulled toward opposing truths, while a war grows from devotion, grief, memory, and love rather than simple hatred. The wolves are not fighting over nothing. They are fighting because both sides are protecting something real.

Beneath that conflict waits Typhon, an enemy who does not merely want the wolves destroyed. He wants them emptied of the things that make them themselves: their names, their bonds, their memories, their certainty that they belong to one another and to their own history. His greatest weapon is not death, but erasure, and the only defense against it is the stubborn refusal to forget.

Standing against that darkness are those who carry the pieces of the truth from different directions. Wulfwyn must discover the wolf who has always been part of her, Eadric must face the cost of centuries spent believing his absence was mercy, Artemis must reckon with everything she preserved while holding his place, and Psyche must decide whether love is worth a road that may lead through death itself. Around them, wolves from distant lands follow a pull they cannot explain, guided by something older than teaching and stronger than fear because their blood remembers what history forgot.

The Cost of Love

Pale Strand, book two. Available now from Amazon and other booksellers.

For more than two centuries, the wolves have lived beneath a name that was never truly theirs, carrying a history altered so completely that almost no one remembers what was lost. When the truth is finally restored, it does not bring peace. It sends a call across the wolf world and awakens loyalties that were never as dead as anyone believed.

Apollo and Artemis have waited longer than most can imagine for this moment, but reclaiming the truth comes with a price. Packs begin to fracture as wolves choose between the lives they know and the blood-deep pull of something older, and neither side believes it is betraying the other.

Because this war is not being born from hatred.

It is being born from love.

Some will fight for the families they built. Some will fight for the history stolen from them. Some will cling to the world that kept them safe, while others will risk everything for the chance to become what they were always meant to be.

And as the first howl rises and the divide becomes impossible to ignore, the wolves will discover that finding the way home may demand more from them than any enemy ever could.

The Cost of Love is the second book in The Pale Strand Trilogy, where ancient truth returns, loyalties splinter, and every heart must decide what it is willing to lose for the thing it loves most.

The Great War

Pale Strand, book three. Available now from Amazon and other booksellers.

Apollo’s true name has been restored after two hundred and thirty years, and the truth has done what lies never could: it has torn the wolf world in two. Not because one side is cruel and the other righteous, but because both are fighting for something they love, something sacred, something they cannot surrender without losing part of themselves.

As war spreads across the territories, old loyalties fracture and ancient wounds split open. Apollo walks into battle carrying guilt that was never his to bear. Artemis watches from the moon, forced to witness the consequences of a devotion that protected one truth while allowing another to disappear. And beneath the ruins of a Roman fort, something far more terrifying than death is being done to captured wolves: they are being stripped of memory, identity, and the names that tell them who they are.

This war will not be measured by the bodies left behind. Its true cost will be paid in memory, in belonging, and in the fragile threads that bind wolves to one another across generations. Because when an enemy learns how to make a people forget themselves, survival is no longer enough.

The wolves must remember who they are before there is nothing left to save.

Even Gods Fall

A romance of the Greek gods. Available now from Amazon and other booksellers.

For two hundred years, Venus believed the worst thing anyone had ever done to her began with Ares.

She was wrong.

The truth should make things easier. Instead, it leaves the goddess of love standing before the god of war with an apology that cannot return two lost centuries, while Ares must decide whether knowing she was wrong is enough to undo what believing him capable of betrayal did to them both.

Ares has survived wars, gods, monsters, and centuries of being exactly what Olympus expected him to be. Distance has always been easier than trust, and anger far safer than wanting anything he could lose. Venus knows better than anyone how convincing beautiful lies can be, including the ones she has told herself.

But some histories refuse to remain buried.

As old wounds reopen and the shadow of Venus’s past reaches for her again, Ares and Venus are forced into something neither of them knows how to navigate: standing on the same side. What begins as a reckoning becomes a battle for survival, then something far more dangerous for two immortals who have spent centuries protecting themselves from exactly this kind of fall.

Because loving a god is not the same as trusting one. Forgiveness is not the same as forgetting. And home may have nothing to do with the mountain where the gods have always ruled.

For the god of war and the goddess of love, the greatest battle will not be fought with weapons.

It will be deciding whether two people who have spent centuries apart can finally choose each other.

Even gods fall. Some of them are better for it.

Blood Lines

A dark comedy of the Greek gods. The companion to Even Gods Fall. Coming soon.

Phobos and Deimos were never supposed to be ordinary children. Born to Ares and Venus, they inherit beauty, divine blood, and enough power to make even Olympus nervous, with Phobos carrying panic and Deimos carrying terror.

Together, they can turn a room, a palace, or an army into chaos without understanding why everyone around them is suddenly unraveling. At first, the destruction is accidental, but it does not stay that way for long.

Between Olympian daycare disasters, impossible escapes, shattered dignity, terrified gods, ruined plans, and a growing list of people who would very much like someone to control them, the boys become the one problem Olympus cannot solve. Ares tries discipline, Venus tries denial, Hermes becomes the worst possible influence, Hera loses patience, and Zeus finds the whole thing far too entertaining.

Then Hades sees what everyone else has missed. The boys are not monsters, and they are not broken.

They are weapons no one ever taught how to aim. Under the one god willing to understand them instead of fear them, Phobos and Deimos begin to change, and raw, joyful destruction becomes precision, strategy, and something far more dangerous than chaos.

Olympus may survive two unruly children. It may not survive the generals they become.

Blood Lines is a darkly funny Greek mythology reimagining about family, fear, power, and two divine boys who were never the problem everyone thought they were.

The Blood of Three Crowns

A paranormal romance. Available now from Amazon and other booksellers.

The three oldest races have spent centuries divided by blood, crown, and hatred, each guarding the power of its own First Blood as if survival depends on keeping the others beneath them. Wolves, dragons, and vampires were never meant to share a future, but prophecy has never cared what kings believe.

Vuk has lived as proof of that truth. Born carrying both wolf and dragon blood, he has been hunted, feared, and forced to hide what he is simply to survive, until fate leaves him bleeding in wolf territory and puts him in the path of Elara.

Elara is the firstborn daughter of an Alpha bloodline, raised beneath the weight of duty, inheritance, and expectations that have already decided what her life should become. Meeting Vuk should be impossible, trusting him should be dangerous, and loving him could threaten every law their world has built around blood.

But something older than law is moving around them. A prophecy speaks of three bloodlines carried in one, of a child no crown can claim, and of an age of division that will end not through conquest, but through a choice powerful enough to change the balance of every realm.

The vampire king believes he can stop that future before it begins. Instead, every attempt to control fate may be driving them closer to the very prophecy he fears.

As ancient rivalries sharpen into war and the truth of Vuk’s blood becomes impossible to hide, Elara must choose between the world she was born to obey and the man who was never supposed to exist. Because if the prophecy is right, their love will not simply unite two lives.

It will create the blood of three crowns.
