The Alpha’s Brother

A werewolf romance. Coming soon.

Ryder Kane has a platinum record, a Harley, and a reputation that gets to the room before he does. He is also the younger brother of the Alpha of Black Heart Pack, the largest and strongest wolf pack in the United States, and the only wolf in line for a title he never wanted.

The road was supposed to keep him free. Then fate hands him a mate who breaks him in front of the whole world, and a second chance he does not trust: Kira Vane, the smart-mouthed lead singer opening his stadium tour, who is not afraid of his name, his wolf, or his past.

A country star. A pack a thousand wolves strong. And a woman who intends to keep him.
